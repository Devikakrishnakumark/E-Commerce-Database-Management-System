About ShopEase

ShopEase is a modern e-commerce platform designed to make online shopping simple, secure, and convenient. This project demonstrates an E-Commerce Database Management System developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Customers can browse products, manage their shopping cart, place orders, and track purchases, while administrators can manage products, customers, inventory, and sales.
